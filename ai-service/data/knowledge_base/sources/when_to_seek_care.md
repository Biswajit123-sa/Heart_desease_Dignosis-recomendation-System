# When to Seek Medical Care

## Warning Signs of a Heart Attack
Warning signs can include chest pain or pressure, discomfort spreading to
the arms, back, neck, jaw, or stomach, shortness of breath, cold sweat,
nausea, and lightheadedness. Symptoms can differ between individuals, and
some people, particularly women, may experience less typical symptoms
such as fatigue or indigestion-like discomfort.

## Emergency Response
Any suspected heart attack symptoms should be treated as a medical
emergency. Seeking immediate emergency care is critical, as prompt
treatment significantly improves outcomes.

## Routine Risk Assessment
Individuals with multiple risk factors (such as smoking, diabetes,
hypertension, high cholesterol, obesity, family history, or physical
inactivity) are encouraged to discuss personalized screening and
prevention plans with a healthcare provider, even in the absence of
symptoms.

## Role of AI-Based Risk Tools
AI-based risk prediction tools can help highlight patterns and risk
factors for further discussion with a clinician, but they are not a
substitute for professional medical evaluation, diagnostic testing, or
clinical judgment.
